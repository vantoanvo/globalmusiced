Our Mission

Global MusicEd is an education-connections service dedicated to music. We partner with distinguished professors and generous sponsors to bring lessons, masterclasses, and scholarships to students around the world.

🎓 Expert Faculty

Professors from leading conservatories and universities.

In-person and online programs across countries and time zones.

Scholarships funded by sponsors who believe in music education.
